# Prompt construction

Transform briefs into model-appropriate prompts without replacing the editorial
idea with generic AI-art language.

Preserve:

- purpose;
- editorial idea;
- subject relationships;
- composition and hierarchy;
- negative space/crop needs;
- visual language;
- avoid list;
- authenticity constraints;
- exact text requirements when intentional.

Prefer explicit concrete direction over adjectives with no visual consequence.
Do not add boilerplate such as `masterpiece`, `award-winning`, `8K`,
`ultra-detailed`, or `trending on ArtStation` unless model-specific configuration
explicitly justifies it.

Do not fabricate real people, events, screenshots, documents, dashboards, or
other evidence when the brief requires authenticity.

When the brief asks for no text, make that constraint explicit. When generated
text is unavoidable or unreliable, warn the caller rather than claiming
accuracy.
