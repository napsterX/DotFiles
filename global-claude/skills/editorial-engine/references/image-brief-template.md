# Image brief

## Purpose

What useful editorial job does this visual perform?

## Editorial idea

What single concept, mechanism, tension, relationship, or implication should the
reader understand from the image?

## Placement

Hero / inline / section opener / other.

## Classification

Explanatory / evidentiary / photographic / editorial-conceptual / infographic.

## Composition

Orientation, framing, hierarchy, focal subject, depth, negative space, crop
requirements, and mobile considerations.

## Visual language

Art direction and publication-specific style. Reference `visual-style.md`.

## Subject requirements

Required objects, people, environment, relationships, actions, proportions, or
spatial cues.

## Avoid

Clichés, unwanted motifs, factual errors, misleading implications, styles,
objects, colors, or composition patterns that would weaken the concept.

## Text in image

Usually `none`. If intentional, specify exact text and why generated typography
is appropriate.

## Aspect ratio

Specify based on placement, for example `16:9`, `4:3`, `3:2`, `1:1`, or `9:16`.

## Authenticity constraints

What must the image not imply, fabricate, impersonate, or present as real
historical/evidentiary material?
